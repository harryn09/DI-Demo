Enrol
every learner, accounted for

Enrol is the national register of student enrolments. Schools and kura use it to record enrolments, transfers between schools, and departures from the education system — keeping an accurate national picture of where every learner is.

Go to Enrol All services
What it does

The national enrolment picture

Enrolments & transfers

Record new enrolments and update records as learners transfer between schools or leave the education system.

Vision & hearing results

Health NZ Vision and Hearing Technicians have restricted access to enter test results directly against learner records.

Attendance visibility

Attendance Service staff have read-only access to support learners who are not enrolled or not attending regularly.

Mandatory training

All users complete training before accessing Enrol, so records are maintained accurately and consistently nationwide.

Who uses it

A shared national register

  • ✓ Schools and kura — full access (mandatory system)
  • ✓ Health NZ Vision & Hearing Technicians — restricted access
  • ✓ Attendance Service staff — read-only access
