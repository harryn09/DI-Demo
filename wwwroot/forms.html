Education Sector Logon / Tertiary access request (Form 120)
ESL Form 120 · March 2026

Tertiary Education
Services access request

Apply for an Education Sector Logon account and request access to on-line educational services for your tertiary organisation — the digital version of ESL Form 120.

Use this form to:

  • Apply for an Education Sector Logon account if you don't already have one
  • Request access to on-line educational services using your ESL account, where an on-line Delegated Authoriser (DA) cannot allocate the access you need

Before you start:

  1. Complete Parts 1, 2 and 4 yourself
  2. Your organisation's Education Sector Authoriser or Delegated Authoriser completes Part 3
  3. Complete Part 5 only if your organisation doesn't have an ESL on-line Delegated Authoriser
  4. Submit the form — you'll be asked to confirm before a summary is sent to the Digital Identity team for processing

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required.
