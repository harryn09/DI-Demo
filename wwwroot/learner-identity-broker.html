Learner Identity Broker
log in once, learn anywhere

A secure digital bridge between learners and online education services. Students sign in with their school account and are connected to every Ministry-approved service their school supports — no separate accounts, no forgotten passwords.

Register your school All services
Key benefits

Less admin, more learning

One login for everything

Learners use a single school login instead of a different password for every platform — simpler for students, teachers and whānau.

Seamless school transfers

When a learner changes schools, their access and records transfer with them — learning continues without interruption.

Safer by design

Centralised authentication means fewer credentials to manage and stronger protection of learner information under the Education Digital Identity framework.

Set up in 10–15 minutes

Schools and kura opt in through a step-by-step registration completed by their IT administrator, with support from the LIB team.

How it works

A digital bridge to every service

  • 1. Your school or kura opts in and registers with the Learner Identity Broker.
  • 2. Learners sign in once with their existing school account.
  • 3. The broker connects them to the approved services your school supports.
  • 4. If they move schools, their access moves with them.
