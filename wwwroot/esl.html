Education Sector Logon
one account, every application

ESL is the identity management, authentication and authorisation solution for the education sector — giving early learning providers, schools and kura, tertiary institutions and Ministry staff secure access to the applications they need.

Go to ESL All services
Key features

Secure access, managed simply

Multi-factor authentication

Protect your account with MFA using the Microsoft or Google Authenticator apps, with evidence-of-identity verification for added assurance.

Delegated Authorisers

Each organisation nominates Delegated Authorisers who create and manage accounts and access for their staff — keeping control local.

Self-service portal

Users manage their own account details, credentials and MFA settings without waiting on a service desk.

Role-based access

Access to each application depends on holding a valid role — the right people see the right systems, and nothing more.
