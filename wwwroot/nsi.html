The sector's learner identity register

National Student Index
one learner, one number, for life

The NSI is the Ministry-maintained register at the heart of learner identity. It assigns every learner a unique National Student Number (NSN) used across early childhood, school and tertiary education — so information stays accurate and privacy stays protected.

Go to the NSI application All services
Key capabilities

Accurate records, sector-wide

NSN allocation

Every learner receives one unique National Student Number that stays with them through their entire education journey.

Search, create & update

Authorised users can search the index, create new learner records, and keep existing records up to date.

Duplicate merging

Duplicate records can be merged so each learner has a single authoritative record, with change notifications for connected users.

Privacy protected

The NSN makes it easier to manage and share student information safely, protecting learner privacy across the sector.

Who uses it

Built for tertiary and beyond

Tertiary Education Organisations receiving EFTS funding, or offering qualifications approved for student loans and allowances, access the NSI:

  • ✓ Through their own Student Management System, or
  • ✓ Directly via the NSI web application
