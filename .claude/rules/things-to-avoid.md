# Things to Avoid

- Light-grey-on-white low-contrast text; tiny fonts.
- Stock photos of generic students; prefer abstract gradient/geometric visuals like the reference.
- Copying resend.com's branding, wording, or exact palette — it is a *style* reference only.
- Feature bloat: every section must serve one of the four services or build trust.
- Inventing service capabilities, statistics, or testimonials beyond what `.claude/rules/services.md` documents (illustrative mock UI content is fine when clearly generic).
