# The Four Services

Factual reference for all service content on the site. Never invent capabilities beyond these.

## Education Sector Logon (ESL)
An identity management, authentication and authorisation solution giving secure single-account access to education sector applications. Serves early learning providers, schools and kura, tertiary institutions, Ministry staff, and attendance service providers. Key features: two user roles (**Delegated Authoriser** — manages accounts for an organisation; **User** — manages their own account), multi-factor authentication (Microsoft/Google Authenticator), a self-service account management portal, and evidence-of-identity verification.
Reference: https://applications.education.govt.nz/education-sector-logon-esl

## Learner Identity Broker (LIB)
A secure "digital bridge" that lets students log in once with their school account and be connected to the Ministry-approved online education services their school supports — no separate accounts per platform. Schools and kura opt in via a 10–15 minute registration by their IT administrator. Benefits: simplified single login for learners, improved security through centralised authentication, and seamless transfer of access and records when a student changes schools. Related terminology: **learner identity**, **Education Digital Identity**.
Reference: https://www.education.govt.nz/education-professionals/schools-year-0-13/digital-technology/learner-identity-broker

## National Student Index (NSI)
The education sector's core learner identity register — a Ministry-maintained database that assigns every learner a unique, lifelong **National Student Number (NSN)** used across early childhood, school, and tertiary education. Used by Tertiary Education Organisations (via their Student Management System or the NSI web application) to allocate NSNs, search/create/update student records, merge duplicates, and receive change notifications. The NSN enables safe sharing of student information while protecting privacy.
Reference: https://applications.education.govt.nz/national-student-index-nsi-web-application

## Enrol
The national register of student enrolments, maintained by schools and kura (mandatory). Records enrolments, transfers between schools, and departures from the education system. Also used by Health NZ Vision and Hearing Technicians (restricted access, test results entry) and Attendance Service staff (read-only). Training is mandatory before access is granted.
Reference: https://applications.education.govt.nz/enrol
