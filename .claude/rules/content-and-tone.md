# Content & Tone

- Plain-language first (aim for reading age ~12 for learner-facing pages); technical depth lives on per-service subpages or a "For integrators" section.
- Professional, warm, and trustworthy — government service voice, not startup hype. Avoid marketing superlatives.
- New Zealand English spelling (enrolment, organisation, centre). Te reo Māori greetings/terms welcome where appropriate (e.g. whānau, ākonga, kura, motu).
- Service facts must come from `.claude/rules/services.md` — do not invent features, statistics, or policies.
